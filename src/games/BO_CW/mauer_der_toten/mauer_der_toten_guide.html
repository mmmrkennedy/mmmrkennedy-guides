CRBR-S

The CRBR-S is a pistol that fires two high-energy shots per ammo used, thanks to the Copy-Cat Orb floating around the gun. It can be Pack-a-Punched and has three variants. Available from the mystery box, trials, or via the following quest:

  1. Assemble the base version of Klaus.
  2. Have Klaus open the Locker located in the 'Switch Control Room' by using the Tactical Equipment to move him in front of it.
  3. Pick up the Blacklight from the Locker.
  4. In the following three locations, a number on a wall is only visible with the blacklight (coloured blue):
    Dial Number Location
    First Dial
    • Garment Factory - On the Chalkboard, left of the Ammo Crate
    • Garment Factory - Above the doorway near the Ammo Crate
    • Garment Factory - On the wall with Graffiti, at the bottom of the small staircase
    Second Dial
    • Service Passage - Above the large doorway in the room with the ladder
    • Service Passage - On the wall below the large pipe, right of the ladder
    • Service Passage - Above the Electrical Box beside the broken wall
    Third Dial
    • Grocery Store - Beside the "Bier Fest" Poster left of the main entrance
    • Grocery Store - On the shelves in the middle of the room
    • Grocery Store - Below the Watermelon Poster, in the corner above the sacks
  5. Once you have all three numbers, go to 'Hotel Room 305' and use them to unlock the Safe.
  6. Once you enter the right combination, the safe will open revealing the CRBR-S ready for pickup.

Swarm, Blazer or Diffuser

  • All variants drop from killing Zombies. There is no way to force a specific variant to drop.
  • Once a variant runs out of ammo, the CRBR-S will return to its base variant.

Klaus

  1. Turn on Power and unlock Pack-a-Punch.
  2. Equip a weapon with the Brain Rot ammo mod from the Pack-a-Punch machine.
  3. Navigate to Hotel Room 305 and spawn a brain-rotted zombie near the Barricaded Door.
  4. The brain-rotted Zombie will tear down the barricade. Once inside, pick up the Robotic Hands from the corpse on the bed.
  5. Kill a Krasny Soldat and pick up the Battery it drops. They spawn in for the first time around round 10.
  6. Navigate to the Safe House and interact with Klaus to install the Hands and Battery.

Klaus Upgrade

  1. Navigate to "Death Strip" (both sides of Pack-a-Punch) and interact with debris piles until the Microwave Dish spawns.
  2. Navigate to the 'Garment Factory' and install the Dish on the Upgrade Station.
  3. Deploy Klaus and let him kill about 45 Zombies. Once he gets enough kills, a sound plays and the monitor on the top-right of the Upgrade Station will turn green.
  4. Command Klaus to enter the Upgrade Station where you must defend him from infinitely spawning enemies until the timer finishes counting down.
  5. Have Klaus open the Locker located in the 'Switch Control Room' by using the Tactical Equipment to move him in front of it. Pick up the Blacklight from the open locker.
  6. Shine the Blacklight on each electrical box below. Two will have a Klaus Symbol. Command Klaus to those two boxes to open them and reveal floppy disks. Box locations:
    • 5th Floor Apartments - Across from Zipline
    • Hotel Lounge - Across from Stamin-Up
    • Ghost Station - Near Service Passage
    • Maintenance Tunnel - Near Safe House
    • West Berlin Street - Near Café Müller
    • Blasted Suite - Near Barrier
  7. Collect the Floppy Disks and return to the Upgrade Station.
  8. Command Klaus to enter the Upgrade Station and interact with it to insert a disk and begin the Upgrade Process. Klaus does not need to be defended during this. Repeat for the other Floppy Disk.
  9. Once both floppy disk upgrades have been completed, Klaus will be fully upgraded.

"Tin Man Heart" Main Quest

Stats (Solo): Difficulty - 5/10, Est. Completion Time - 40 Mins to 1 Hr

  1. Prerequisites

  2. Turn on Power and unlock Pack-a-Punch.
  3. Assemble Klaus.

    Upgraded Klaus is required for Step . Un-upgraded Klaus is required for Step .

  4. Obtain the CRBR-S.

  5. Find the Secret Lab

  6. Craft the Hacking Helm. There are three parts, two of which have fixed locations. Shoot the objects below with the CRBR-S to make them drop. Locations:
    • 'Apartment Rooftop' - Radio Tower
    • 'Ghost Station' - Box on the wall, near the M16 wallbuy
    • 'Electronics Store' - Boombox: Location 1, Location 2, Location 3
  7. Once all three items have been collected, navigate to the Safe House and craft the Hacking Helm on Klaus.
  8. Navigate to Sewer Access and use the Klaus Remote Control to Command Klaus to break this brick wall to reveal the metal wall.
  9. Obtain the Blazer Mod Kit (Laser Beam Variant of the CRBR-S) and shoot the Metal Wall till it melts.

  10. Charge the Conversion Unit

  11. Once a player enters the lab, the current round will end and a Tormentor round will begin.
  12. Once the Tormentor round is over, collect the three empty canisters from the Conversion Unit.
  13. Shoot this machine with the CRBR-S to open it. Once open, the player can pick up an Endstation Lure.
  14. Find three harvesters in the locations below. Only three of the seven harvesters spawn each game, so some spots will be empty. Locations:
    • West Berlin Street
    • Destroyed Penthouse (Below Quick Revive)
    • East Berlin Street
    • Korber Rooftop
    • Alley
    • Sewer Access
    • Ghost Station
  15. Insert the canister into the harvester, then throw the lure near it to spawn Tempests. Kill the Tempests near the harvester to charge the canister. Once all Tempests are dead, pick up the canister. If it can't be picked up, kill more Tempests with another lure.
  16. Bring the filled canister to the Conversion Unit and deposit it.
  17. Repeat Steps for the other two Canisters.

  18. Stop the Train

  19. Navigate to the 'Switch Control Room' and interact with the Railway Switch.
  20. Fully upgrade Klaus.
  21. Command Klaus to stand on the track furthest from Mule Kick near the M16 wallbuy. When the train comes, Klaus will stop it so you can board. If Klaus fails, wait until the next round, flip the Railway Switch, and try again.
  22. On the train, the player must quickly pick up Rico's Keycard and the Bomb before the train leaves.
  23. Return to the Secret Lab and interact with the Conversion Unit to place the Bomb.

  24. Obtain the Uranium

  25. Navigate to the Safe House and interact with this computer until the screen shows a satellite.
  26. Navigate to the Objective Marker and kill the HVT Megaton. Two Uranium Rocks will drop from the Megaton.
  27. Pick one up and bring it to one of the workbenches to craft a Uranium Device.
    • West Berlin Street
    • Military Tent
  28. Navigate to the Destroyed Penthouse Zipline and attach the Uranium Device.
  29. Repeat Step for the other Radioactive Material and then navigate to the Korber Rooftop Zipline and attach the Uranium Device.
  30. Both Uranium Devices will shoot across the zipline, collide in the middle, and land on the street below. Collect the Cleansed Uranium and return it to the conversion unit.

  31. Once the second Uranium is returned to the conversion unit, the Boss Fight will start.

  32. Repeat Steps to get another Cleansed Uranium.

  33. Valentina Boss Fight

  34. Valentina has three attacks, two damage dealing and one healing. They are:
    Attack What Happens
    Fire (Damage) Three orange crystals appear over Valentina's head and begin shooting at a player. The projectiles can be outrun but not avoided while walking.
    Wipe (Damage) A blue aura will appear around the player's screen. All players must take cover (out of view of Valentina) to avoid being instantly downed.
    Heal Valentina will spawn floating zombies surrounding her. Kill the zombies to stop her from healing.
  35. To attack Valentina, shoot her until she becomes immune and moves to a new area. She will move to four different areas before returning to the secret lab.
  36. Once Valentina's health bar hits 0, a cutscene will play then the player must defend Klaus as he walks the Bomb into the Portal.
  37. Once he makes it into the portal, the game will end and the final cutscene will start.

Free Aether Tool (Weapon Tier Upgrade)

  1. Turn on Power
  2. Navigate to the 'Apartment Rooftop.'
  3. To spawn the targets, interact with the Folder on the ground in the corner closest to the street.
  4. Five targets will spawn in the following locations one at a time. This is timed, and you will not be allowed to retry if you fail to shoot all the targets. The order of the targets is always the same. The targets move left to right. Target locations:
    1. Königshaus Sign - Zoomed Out --- Zoomed In
    2. Windows of Königshaus building - Zoomed Out --- Zoomed In
    3. Korber sign - Zoomed Out --- Zoomed In
    4. Windows of Der Dozent building - Zoomed Out --- Zoomed In
    5. Der Dozent sign - Zoomed Out --- Zoomed In
  5. Once the last target is shot, an Aether Tool will spawn nearby.

Mister Peeks' Nightclub (Free Loot)

  1. Collect six pieces of Mister Peeks (Aether Bunny) from the locations below:
    • 'Sewer Access' - Near wooden pallet
    • 'Department Store' - Right of Wunderfizz on Shelf
    • 'Grocery Store' - Shelf
    • 'Bar' - Left of Juggernog
    • 'Alley' - Near tire stack
    • 'Hotel Room 304' - Desk
  2. Upon collecting the last piece, all players will be teleported to Mister Peeks' Nightclub.
  3. Zombies attack in waves. After three waves, Mister Peeks disappears and you can collect a random reward from one of three doors.
  4. Every 15 rounds, you can return to the nightclub by going to East Berlin Streets and shooting Mister Peeks on the window ledge of the "Hasenbau" building.

Free Loot

  1. Navigate to the 'East Berlin Streets' and locate the exposed sewer entrance found on the ground near a military vehicle.
  2. Throw explosive equipment or shoot explosive weapons into the hole until loot drops.
  3. This can be repeated up to three times in a single match.
