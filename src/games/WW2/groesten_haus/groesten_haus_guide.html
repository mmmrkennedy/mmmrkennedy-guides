Mystery Box Room

  • Shoot ten lanterns around the map. When a lantern is shot correctly, it will very faintly light up purple and a raven's caw sound will play. Lantern Locations:

    1. Downstairs

    2. Hanging at the bottom of the stairs, left of the MP-40 wallbuy
    3. Outside the window left of the MP-40 wallbuy, on the hood of a truck
    4. Hanging on the support column in the middle of the room, right of the MP-40 wallbuy
    5. At the bottom of the stairs in the Kitchen
    6. Above the table in the Kitchen
    7. Outside the window in the Kitchen, on the metal cart, left of the stack of wood

    8. Upstairs

    9. Hanging above the top of the stairs closest to the MP-40 wallbuy
    10. Across from the Random Blitz machine
    11. On a chair, left of the Combat Shotgun wallbuy
    12. On the desk, across from the Combat Shotgun wallbuy

  • Pack-A-Punched Weapons from Mystery Box

    1. Obtain Jack-In-The-Boxes from the Mystery Box.
    2. Throw a Jack-In-The-Box at the metal square on top of a beam above the staircase near the Blitz machine. Once the Jack-in-the-Box blows up, the metal square should be gone.
    3. Give the piano next to the Mystery Box room 10,000 Jolts.
    4. All weapons from the Mystery Box will be Pack-A-Punched.

Unlockable Characters

New playable characters can be unlocked through the completion of various challenges. Your progress can be checked on the Lobby Menu → Soldier → Dossier → Challenges. All the challenge requirements have been confirmed via modding, and reading the game's code. The challenges listed here will/may differ from those found else where online.
