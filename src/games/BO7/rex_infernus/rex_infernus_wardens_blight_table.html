Warden's Blight Quotes and Lever Combinations

The Warden's Blight Quote step in Rex Infernus has Dravakar speak one of four riddles. Each quote tells you how many times to interact with the left, middle, and right levers. Match the line you heard to the table below, then interact with each lever the number of times listed beside it.

Text Levers to Interact with

"I drift to the runner that travels moons, who borrow from galaxies when stars stay true."
  • Left Lever - 3 times
  • Middle Lever - 2 times
  • Right Lever - 1 time

"I remember the runner that travels to stars, while moons and galaxies stay true."
  • Middle Lever - 2 times
  • Right Lever - 3 times

"I drift to stars that remember moons, who borrow the runner that travels galaxies."
  • Left Lever - 1 time
  • Middle Lever - 2 times
  • Right Lever - 2 times

"I remember galaxies that drift to moons, who borrow the runner that travels the stars."
  • Left Lever - 2 times
  • Right Lever - 2 times
