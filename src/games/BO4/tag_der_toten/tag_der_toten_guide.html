Power

There are a total of three Power Switches in the map, each activating power for its corresponding area.

  • Docks
  • Bridge
  • Human Infusion (Zipline Handle required)

Pack-a-Punch

  1. Activate Power at the Docks and Bridge.
  2. In the 'Lighthouse Approach' cave, shoot or melee the Blue Rock (Location 1, Location 2 or Location 3) to knock it to the ground and pick it up.
  3. Navigate to 'Lighthouse Level 4' and place the rock on the Pulley that the Hermit lowers down.
  4. Occasionally, the lighthouse will shine its light on a location on the map. This will cause the Pack-a-Punch machine to appear there for a limited amount of time (~5 Mins).

Golden Pack-a-Punch

Golden Pack-a-Punch upgrades a weapon to its highest level immediately, instead of requiring four uses like normal PAP.

  1. Activate Pack-a-Punch and obtain the Zipline Handle.
  2. Activate Power Switch in 'Human Infusion.'
  3. Occasionally, the lighthouse won't shine at a normal location, but at an iceberg off the side of the ship. This is also indicated by a different sound cue from the one that plays when the PAP moves.
  4. All players must stand on the Flinger on the 'Sun Deck' to be flung to the iceberg. While there, the Golden Pack-a-Punch costs 5000 points (or 2500/1500 on an already PAPed weapon) and Zombies spawn infinitely. After about two minutes, all players are flung back to the ship.

Flinger Repairs

  • There are two flingers around the map, and both need their gearboxes repaired by the Hermit.
  • Pick up a gearbox (if you can't, make sure Power is on for that location) and give it to the Hermit. He'll repair it and hand it back. Return the gearbox to the Flinger to activate it.
    • Navigation
    • Facility (You will need the Zipline Handle to get to the Facility, and a Dynamite Bomb to clear the debris)

Thundergun

The Thundergun is a wonder weapon that fires blasts of compressed air strong enough to wipe out an entire horde in one shot. It can be Pack-a-Punched without completing an Easter Egg. It can also be obtained via the Mystery Box or the following quest:

  1. Complete all five Hermit Challenge Totems.
  2. Navigate to 'Lighthouse Level 4' and collect the Key from the Hermit's pulley.
  3. Interact with the Reward Crate left of the Hermit's Pulley on 'Lighthouse Level 4' to open it, revealing a free Thundergun.

Tundragun

The Tundragun is a modified Thundergun that fires snowballs. It does heavy splash damage to you, so PhD Slider is highly recommended. It can be upgraded via Pack-a-Punch and obtained via the Mystery Box or the following quest:

  1. Obtain the Zipline Handle and Repair the Flinger on the Boat.
  2. Hit five targets with snowballs in a specific order within 1 Min and 45 Secs. A successful hit makes a ding sound. If you take too long or hit the wrong target, you start over from the first. Target Locations and Order:
    1. Loading Platform - Above doorway to inside Facility
    2. Lagoon - Near the bottom of the Ice Slide
    3. Sun Deck - Just outside the room with the power switch
    4. Lighthouse Level 3 - On the center pole of the spiral staircase
    5. Cargo Hold - On a support beam
  3. If done correctly, the Wooden Box, left of the last target, will open to reveal the Tundragun for you to pick up.

Wunderwaffe DG-Scharfschütze

The Wunderwaffe DG-Scharfschütze is a "sniper" that fires electricity chaining to nearby enemies. Headshots amplify its power, killing faster and chaining to more enemies per shot. It can be upgraded via Pack-a-Punch and obtained via the Mystery Box or the following quest:

  1. Shoot down and pick up an Icicle in one of the following locations:
    • Beach - On the Cave Entrance on the way to the Lighthouse
    • Lighthouse Station - On the 'Lighthouse Level 4' broken railing
    • Lighthouse Cove - On the stairs above
  2. Navigate to the 'Forecastle' and place the Icicle into the Pot above the Campfire. Once the Icicle has melted, collect the Key from the Pot.
  3. Obtain the Zipline Handle.
  4. Interact with the Safe in 'Specimen Storage' to open it, revealing the Vril Device. Kill Zombies near the Safe until the Vril Device starts to glow.
  5. Give the Vril Device to the Hermit, and in return, he'll give you the Reward Crate Key.
  6. Interact with the Reward Crate left of the VAPR-XKG wallbuy on 'Lighthouse Level 4' to open it, revealing a free Wunderwaffe DG-Scharfschütze.

Samantha's Music Box

  1. Obtain the Zipline Handle and activate all three Power Switches.
  2. Around the Facility, find two key cards in the following locations:
    Key Card Locations
    Key Card 1
    • Decontamination - In the dryer at the bottom of the stairs coming from the 'Security Lobby'
    • Decontamination - In one of the eye flush stations in front of the Tonic Perk Machine
    • Decontamination - In the sink, left of the Tonic Perk Machine
    Key Card 2

    • Specimen Storage - In the toilet of the left-most cell
    • Specimen Storage - On the bottom shelf of the canister rack, right of the buildable table
  3. Interact with the Security Panel in the 'Security Lobby' to insert the Key Cards. Wait a few seconds, then pick the Key Cards back up.
  4. In the 'Human Infusion' area, insert the Key Cards into the card readers on both sides of the Vault Door.
  5. Interact with the Vault Door to start a lockdown. Once the vault door is fully opened, Samantha's Music Box can be obtained from the vault box found within. Once you pick up the Music Box, the lockdown will end.
    • Samantha's Music Box will replace the current lethal equipment like Wraith Fires, Monkey Bombs, etc. Picking up snowballs will not replace the Music Box; you'll get it back once you run out of snowballs.

Dynamite Bomb

  1. Wait for a Flaming Zombie to spawn.
    • Flaming Zombies only spawn when you don't have a crafted Dynamite Bomb or all three required parts in your inventory.
  2. Kill it with Snowballs (always a one-shot headshot kill) or the Tundragun to make it drop a part.
  3. Repeat two more times for two more parts.
  4. Once you have all three parts, the Dynamite Bomb can only be crafted in the Gangway.

Heat Pack

  1. Obtain the Zipline Handle and a Dynamite Bomb, and activate all three Power Switches.
  2. Make sure the 'Frozen Crevasse,' 'Lagoon,' and 'Lighthouse Cove' areas are opened.
  3. Repair the Flinger on the 'Outer Walkway' of the Facility.
  4. Depending on where you stand on the Flinger (left, middle, right), you'll be flung to one of three locations. You need to land on each one, as landing on a box breaks it and reveals a Part. Box locations:
    • Left (Red) - Frozen Crevasse
    • Middle (Green) - Lagoon
    • Right (Blue) - Lighthouse Cove
  5. Craft the Heat Pack in the Lighthouse Station or Specimen Storage.

Zipline Handle

  1. Obtain a Zipline Crank, which can be found on the 'Stern' in one of the following locations:
    • Stern - Inside the enclosed area, left of the Zombie spawn
    • Stern - Outside the enclosed area, left of the Soda Perk Machine
    • Stern - Leaning against the side of the ship, right of the Bowie Knife
  2. Place the Zipline Crank on the Zipline Box on the 'Sun Deck' and interact with it until the crank stops. This moves the Zipline from the 'Sun Deck' to the 'Beach' into position.
  3. Find another Zipline Crank, which can be found on the 'Forecastle' in one of the following locations:
    • Forecastle - Left of the Mystery Box
    • Forecastle - Left of the Auger DMG wallbuy
    • Forecastle - Right of a Zombie spawn, near the zipline to 'Lighthouse Level 4'
  4. Place the Zipline Crank on the Zipline Box on the 'Main Deck' and interact with it until the crank stops. This moves the Zipline from 'Lighthouse Level 4' to 'Cargo Hold' into position.
  5. Once both Ziplines are in place, return to the Hermit. He'll give you the Zipline Handle.

"Salvation Lies Above" Main Quest

Stats (Solo): Difficulty - 5/10, Est. Completion Time - 1:30 Hr to 1:45 Hr, RNG Rating - 1/5 (The offerings are random, but you don't have to burn rounds to get them)

  1. Ascend from Darkness

  2. Activate Pack-a-Punch (Not Required).
  3. Complete any two Challenge Totems.

  4. Knowledge is Power

  5. Return to the Hermit; he will present you with a set of Dials for you to pick up.
  6. In 'Artifact Storage,' place the dials on four different panels. There are two ways to do this:
    • The Easy Way: Interact with each dial to spin it. When a dial is in the correct position, it will make a ding sound. Do this for each dial.
    • The "Intended" Way: Around the map, there will be coloured numbers drawn on walls. Each number corresponds to the position a dial should be in. There is absolutely no reason to do the step this way, but here are the number locations anyway:
      Number Locations
      Purple Number
      • Gangway - On the wall, by the leftmost booth
      • Bridge - Behind some monitors that are hanging from the ceiling
      • Stern - Inside the enclosed area, on the other side of the wall of the Bowie Knife
      Yellow Number
      • Stern - Outside the enclosed area, near the Soda Perk Machine
      • Navigation - Above and to the right of the Flinger
      • Sun Deck - Right of the door to the 'Bridge,' near the Flinger
      Blue Number
      • Cargo Hold - On the flaming shipping container in the water
      • Cargo Hold - On the opening, above the Spitfire wallbuy
      • Cargo Hold - On the left crane, above the ship
      Orange Number
      • Forecastle - Inside a red shipping container, near the zipline to 'Lighthouse Level 4'
      • Forecastle - On the underside of the crane, closest to the Auger DMR wallbuy
      • Forecastle - On the underside of the Crow's Nest, above the Mystery Box location

  7. Acquire the Vessel

  8. TIP: Turn on subtitles to make the next step easier. Once the dials are set, the Apothicon Blood will start speaking. After a while it will say "Find Us Offerings Three", then give a phrase hinting at an item's location. Pick up the item and it will give you another riddle. Repeat three times total.   9. Once all three items are found, the Apothicon Blood will tell you to "Seek the Seal of Duality in the walls where..." followed by another riddle.
    • "Where humans suffer": Melee the Bulletin Board on the wall by the mailboxes in 'Specimen Storage' to reveal a safe.
    • "Inside an icy hall": Melee the Wooden Board in the 'Ice Grotto' to reveal a safe.
    • "Where Aether was gathered": Melee the Bulletin Board in 'Geological Processing' to reveal a safe.
    • "Where cages hang": Melee the Framed Map in the 'Boathouse' to reveal a safe.
  10. Once the safe is revealed, place a Dynamite Bomb on it to open it, revealing the Seal of Duality for you to pick up.

  11. Tame that which is Bonded

  12. Do not interact with the Apothicon Blood Orb in 'Artifact Storage' unless instructed to. If you do, you will be instantly downed or your health will be dropped down to 50 HP.

  13. Place the Seal on the barrel beneath the Apothicon Blood Orb in 'Artifact Storage.' The Orb will start moving around the room. Shoot it until your screen flashes white and the Orb returns to the centre of the room.
    • Watch the Orb closely as you shoot it. Three orange lines will shoot off from it, pointing to the general locations of the Orbs for the next step.
  14. While you were shooting the Orb, three smaller orbs were launched around the map. Once you find one, don't get close to it. Instead, throw a snowball at it to turn it blue, then shoot it to send it back to the bigger Orb.
    • The small Orbs should always be within the bounds of the map.
    • There are no set locations for the Orbs, but here are some of the more common locations:
      • Lagoon
      • Lighthouse Cove
      • Beach
      • In and around the Facility
      • Hidden Path
      • Frozen Crevasse
  15. Once you've found all three orbs, return to 'Artifact Storage.' The Orbs will be floating near the ceiling. Throw snowballs at them to turn them blue, then shoot them into the Seal. With all three inside, pick up the Seal.
  16. Obtain Samantha's Music Box. The Music Box is required throughout the Easter Egg, so do not replace it with other equipment.
  17. Place the Seal on top of the Campfire in the 'Sunken Path' area and throw a Music Box at it to turn the flame blue and trigger some story dialogue. All Zombies will despawn until it's done. Once it finishes, pick the Seal back up.

  18. Knowledge Itself is for the Taking

  19. Retrieve two Soapstones from the Hermit, place them on the Scorch Marks in the 'Boathouse,' then activate the Lighthouse trap to light them on fire.
  20. Pick up the flaming stones and, while avoiding walking in water, bring them to 'Human Infusion' and place one of them in the terminal in the middle of the room.
  21. Place the other one on the "freeze marks" under the 'Decontamination' room trap. Activate the trap to freeze the stone. Pick up the stone and bring it to the terminal.
  22. Once both different temperature stones are placed in the terminal, a Fuse in the middle of the terminal will become available for pickup.
  23. Place the fuse in the Fuse Box next to the large door in the 'Lighthouse Station' (the one from the OG COTD Easter Egg).
  24. Obtain the Wunderwaffe DG-Scharfschütze.
  25. Using the Wunderwaffe, shoot two red batteries on electricity pylons in the Facility area. Locations:
    • Facility Entrance - Off to the left side of the Facility
    • Loading Platform - Above and behind the Wack-a-mole game
  26. Once the batteries are shot, power three generators by killing an Electric Zombie near each one. Generator Locations:
    • Lighthouse Station - At the top of the stairs leading into the Lighthouse
    • Lighthouse Approach - Behind the MX9 wallbuy
    • Boathouse - Right of Pack-a-Punch location
  27. Once all three have been powered, interact with the Fuse Box in the 'Lighthouse Station' again to open the door. Behind the door is the Elemental Shard for you to pick up.

  28. Tame that which is Merged

  29. End the round, then place the Seal on the barrel beneath the Apothicon Blood Orb in 'Artifact Storage.' It will start moving around the room again. Shoot it until your screen flashes white, and the orb returns to the middle of the room.
  30. Find three orbs around the map again. Once you spot one, don't get close to it. Instead, throw a snowball at it to turn it blue, then shoot it to send it back to the bigger Orb.
  31. Place the Seal on the Campfire in the 'Sunken Path' and throw a Music Box at it to turn the flame blue and trigger story dialogue. Once it's done, pick the Seal back up.

  32. Once you give the Seal to the Hermit, you will not be able to Pack-a-Punch at all for the rest of the match. You will still be able to do everything else (buy perks, build equipment, etc.) just not use a Pack-a-Punch Machine.

  33. Give the Seal of Duality to the Hermit. This starts a lockdown in the Lighthouse with infinitely spawning Zombies for about two minutes. You don't need to kill anything, just wait it out.
  34. Once the Lockdown is over, your screen will go white and the Hermit will return the Seal of Duality to you via the Pulley.

  35. Empower Through the Machines of the Angels

  36. Find where the Pack-a-Punch is and insert the Seal into it. Kill Zombies nearby to charge it. Once enough are killed, the Seal will pop back out.
  37. Repeat this for the other three standard PAP machines. Once you finish at one, the Lighthouse will move to the next PAP machine.

  38. Tame that which is Powered

  39. Once you've charged the Seal at all four PAP machines, return to 'Artifact Storage' and place the Seal on the barrel beneath the Apothicon Blood Orb.
  40. Repeat the Orb step one more time, but this time the smaller Orbs won't leave 'Artifact Storage.' Once all three are in the Seal, pick it back up.
  41. Place the Seal on the Campfire in the 'Sunken Path' and throw a Music Box at it to turn the flame blue and trigger story dialogue. Once it's done, pick the Seal back up.

  42. Salvation Lies Above

  43. Once the Seal is inserted into the Golden PAP, the Boss Fight will start.

  44. Make your way to the Golden PAP iceberg and insert the Seal into it. Kill Zombies nearby to charge the Seal.
  45. Once the Seal has been charged, the Seal will float into the air and transform the map, with any freezing water around the map being turned into lava. It will also create a bubble around it, which you must stay inside to avoid taking damage.

  46. The Infinite / Boss Fight

  47. The Seal will slowly move around the map, stopping three times at the 'Main Deck,' 'Lighthouse Cove,' and 'Lighthouse Station.' At each stop, kill Zombies nearby to charge it. Once charged, the screen flashes white, Max Ammo and Carpenter power-ups spawn, and the Seal moves on.
  48. After three stops, the Seal heads to the top of the Lighthouse, crosses the zipline to the Facility, and finally reaches 'Human Infusion,' where it stops and needs to be charged again.
    • While the Seal is crossing the zipline, try to use the zipline when the Seal is about halfway there to avoid spending too much time outside the bubble on either side.
    • While moving through the Facility, the Seal will often try to "fake you out" with the direction it is going (it will start moving one way before going the other way).
    • The path it takes in the Facility is: 'Facility Entrance' => 'Security Lobby' => 'Decontamination' => right-side walkway in 'Upper Catwalk' => 'Human Infusion'
  49. While charging the Seal, the protection bubble slowly shrinks, so charge it as fast as possible.
  50. Once the Seal is fully charged, Zombies stop spawning and dialogue begins. Wait for it to finish, pick up the Seal, and give it to the Hermit in the Lighthouse. Take the zipline from 'Lighthouse Station' to the 'Sun Deck' and watch the Lighthouse.
  51. After some dialogue, the Seal falls from the sky and lands at the front of the 'Forecastle', between the Mystery Box location and the campfire. Pick it up to start the final cutscene.
  52. After the cutscene, Samantha and Eddie appear in a black void. Hold forward to walk them toward the light while each of the eight main characters speaks. Once you reach the light, the screen goes white and the game ends.

"Don't Freeze" Mode / Teleport to Round 200

When you enter Freeze Mode, the round is set to 200 and all players are locked in the 'Docks'/spawn room.

  1. Freeze yourself in the following ten areas around the map in any order, at any time. To freeze, stand in the water until your screen ices over and you can't move (the more submerged you are, the faster it happens, so crouching helps). If a location has multiple bodies of water (like the 'Sunken Path'), you only need to freeze in one. Water locations:
    • Docks
    • Frozen Crevasse
    • Lagoon
    • Ice Grotto (Bottom of the Ice Slide)
    • Lighthouse Cove
    • Hidden Path
    • Cargo Hold
    • Artifact Storage
    • Sunken Path (To access, either shoot this pipe in 'Artifact Storage' or place a Dynamite Bomb on this debris in the 'Lagoon')
    • Beach
  2. Once you've frozen in all ten pools, a button will appear in the 'Docks.'

  3. Once all players interact with the button, you will be locked in the 'Docks' area for the remainder of the game.

  4. All players must interact with the button to enter Freeze Mode. All paths out of the 'Docks' will be blocked, and at the end of the current round, the round changes to 200. Most importantly, if you stand still anywhere in the arena, you'll start to freeze. Sprint to avoid it.
  5. Completing Round 200 takes about 1 hour and 3500-3600 kills solo.

The Hermit Jumpscare

  1. Obtain a scoped weapon (the Wunderwaffe DG-Scharfschütze does work for this). The Koshka wallbuy can be found in the 'Security lobby.'
  2. Navigate to the 'Facility Entrance' and aim at the Light from the Lighthouse.

George Romero Tribute / Free 500 Points

  • Hold interact on the Pair of Glasses on the cabinet by the stairs in the 'Lighthouse Station.' Your character should say something about them, and you will be given 500 points.

"The Secret" Easter Egg/Achievement

  1. Equip the 'Talkin' Bout Regeneration' Elixir (RECOMMENDED) and/or the 'Dying Wish' Perk to your loadout.
  2. Obtain the Heat Pack and complete the Beach Challenge Totem.
  3. Make sure you have Dying Wish and your Specialist Weapon charged and 'Talkin' Bout Regeneration' ready.
  4. Make your way to the Facility Flinger, crouch, then walk on to the back-left of the Flinger and spin as fast as you can, while remaining crouched (increase controller sensitivity to at least 8).
  5. If done correctly, you'll be launched to a faraway iceberg. Activate your elixir and start swimming. Follow the path through the tunnels to the end. After a while, you'll start taking damage from lack of air.
    • If you have the elixir active, you will heal right back up.
    • If you don't have the elixir, keep swimming till Dying Wish activates, then near the end of it, pull out your specialist weapon for the extra armour.
  6. Once you reach the end, you'll exit the water and find the Secret. There's no physical reward other than an achievement. Interact with the Dock to return to the normal map.
