Power

  • There is one power switch. It's in the room behind the Soda Perk.

Pack-a-Punch

You can still enter the 'Panic Room' after placing the Teleporter Signal Amplifier by activating the Defcon switches and then entering the teleporter at the back of the 'Server Room.'

  1. Craft the Teleporter Signal Amplifier, three parts are required:
    Part Locations
    Teleporter Pad
    • Main Offices - Under desk near Teleporter
    • Main Offices - Top of the fallen desk, near the Mystery Box location
    • Main Offices - Left of the Brew machine
    Amplifier
    1. Activate the four Defcon switches in any order:
      • Server Room - Left of the room
      • War Room: Lower Level - Left of the elevator to the Labs
      • War Room: Upper Level - Right of the elevator to the Offices
      • War Room: Upper Level - Right of the Spitfire wallbuy
    2. Enter the teleporter in the 'War Room: Lower Level' to go to the 'Panic Room.'
    3. Collect the Amplifier.
    Wires
    • Morgue - Top of chair, right of Mystery Box location
    • Morgue - Corner of countertop
    • Morgue - On a body chamber
  2. Craft the Teleporter Signal Amplifier at Central Filing or Weapon Testing.
  3. Place the Teleporter Signal Amplifier on the small teleporter in the 'War Room: Lower Level.'
  4. Once the Defcon level is set to five, the big teleporter in the middle of the 'War Room: Lower Level' will take you to 'Groom Lake' where Pack-a-Punch is.

Winter's Howl (Project Skadi)

The Winter's Howl can be Pack-a-Punched and doesn't require an Easter Egg to do so. The Winter's Howl is available through the mystery box and via the following quest:

  1. Recommended: Equip a Frag Grenade and complete the Nova Crawler Easter Egg on Round 1 to get 6000 points for free.
  2. To get the Winter's Howl, collect four sets of numbers, each corresponding to an image of a previous map. You can collect them in any order.

  3. Der Riese Numbers

  4. Pick up the Key in the box opposite the Cola Perk, in the 'War Room: Lower Level.'
  5. Go to the 'Main Offices' and interact with the desk in the center of the room to open the drawer. A picture of Der Riese with numbers will be inside. Note the numbers.

  6. Shangri-La Numbers

  7. Activate Power.
  8. If you don't have Frag Grenades equipped, purchase and Pack-a-Punch the M1897 Trebuchet from the wallbuy in the 'War Room: Lower Level' near the lower elevator.
  9. In the 'South Laboratories,' navigate to the barrier near 'Weapon Testing.' Throw a Grenade/Shoot a Trebuchet shot behind this machine.
  10. If done correctly, a sound will play and the Shangri-la picture will spawn on the right side of the barrier.

  11. Kino der Toten Numbers

  12. Open Pack-a-Punch.
  13. Activate the Defcon Switches in the following order:
    1. War Room: Upper Level - Right of the Spitfire wallbuy
    2. Server Room - Left of the Room
    3. War Room: Upper Level - Right of the elevator to the Offices
    4. War Room: Lower Level - Left of the elevator to the Labs
  14. Enter the 'Panic Room' via the teleporter in the 'Server Room.'
  15. Interact with this TV to make it slide back, revealing the Kino der Toten numbers below where it was.

  16. Shi No Numa Numbers

  17. Obtain any Pack-a-Punched weapon (the Pack-a-Punched Welling you get while down on solo works).
  18. In the 'Deserted Hallway,' there are four pictures of historical American Figures. Shoot their nameplates in the following order:
    1. George Washington (1st from left)
    2. Benjamin Franklin (3rd from left)
    3. Alexander Hamilton (2nd from left)
    4. Abraham Lincoln (4th from left)
  19. If done correctly, George Washington's nameplate will rise, revealing the Shi No Numa Numbers.

  20. Input the Numbers

  21. In the 'War Room: Lower Level,' there is a machine with flashing pictures and a number pad.
  22. Shoot the numbers to change them, then press the button on the right to enter the code. After each correct entry, the light above the button turns green and the map picture appears on-screen.
  23. You must enter the numbers from the pictures in the following order:
    1. Deserted Hallway Paintings (Shi No Numa)
    2. Main Offices Drawer (Der Riese)
    3. South Laboratories Barrier (Shangri-La)
    4. Panic Room TV (Kino der Toten)
  24. If you enter the wrong number, you must restart with the Shi No Numa code.
  25. If done correctly, a voice and the machine's screen will say "Project Skadi Retrieved" after a few seconds.
  26. Teleport to 'Groom Lake' and survive three full rounds without leaving. This will open the barriers on the left of Pack-a-Punch.
  27. The Winter's Howl can be picked up from the blue glowing case.

Main Quest

Stats (Solo): Difficulty - 9/10, Est. Completion Time - 4 Hrs to 6 Hrs

Recommended Loadout (Anything not listed is your choice)
Perks Special Weapons Elixirs Equipment
  • Victorious Tortoise
  • Secret Sauce (PhD Slider if you get the Hellion Salvo, Dying Wish otherwise)
  • Stonecold Stronghold
  • Timeslip (Modifier)
  • Ragnarok DG-5
  • 'Temporal Gift'
  • 'Equip Mint'
  • Wraith Fire
  1. Unlike every other Main Quest, there's only one step: Reach Round 150. The cutscene triggers after finishing Round 149. Round 150 strategy:
    • Build the Riot Shield in 'Weapon Testing.' You will be using the Shield, Wraith Fire and the Ragnarok DG-5 to get kills.
    • Stand in this corner next to the Shield workbench while holding your shield out.
    • Plant your Ragnarok DG-5s as soon as they're ready. Otherwise, throw a Wraith Fire at your feet.
    • When your Wraith Fires and Ragnaroks aren't ready, shoot the zombies with the Shield gun until a Wraith Fire or the Ragnaroks recharge.
    • Use Equip Mint whenever you have it and you run out of Wraith Fires.
    • Don't let your Shield break. Buy a new one from the bench before it gets low on health or ammo.
    • If you'd rather use the Hellion Salvo, swap Dying Wish for PhD Slider and shoot the Hellion at your feet until the Ragnaroks recharge. Return to using the Shield when the Hellion runs out of ammo.

Last Gen Richtofen Jump Scare

  1. Open Pack-a-Punch
  2. Obtain a sniper with a default scope. The Koshka is on the wall in 'Weapon Testing' for 4500 points.
  3. Navigate to 'Groom Lake' and aim at this light outside the map, left of Pack-a-Punch.

Nova Crawler Easter Egg / Free ~6,000 Points

  • In the 'Main Offices,' interact with this door left of the Brew Perk Machine five times.
    • Interacting with the door will play a knocking sound, and Robert McNamara will speak to you.
    • After the fifth interaction, Samantha will yell at you and 40 Nova Crawlers will spawn. Kill them all with melee for about 6000 points. Use Double Points and Temporal Gift Elixirs to push that to 12,000.
